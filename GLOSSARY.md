# Fluidtime

Fluidtime tracks work recorded in Solidtime and summarizes progress toward work targets.

## Language

**Daily target**:
The desired amount of tracked work for a day. A zero target means no daily target is set.

**Remaining daily work**:
The amount of tracked work still needed to reach the daily target, never less than zero.

**Expected finish**:
The projected time at which the daily target will be reached, assuming uninterrupted work from now. It includes work already tracked today and makes no allowance for future breaks.
